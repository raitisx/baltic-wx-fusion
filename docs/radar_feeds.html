Per-radar debug

One row per country: the product as served (SOURCE, untouched), then our render of it, then the radars. Sweden and Finland publish one product PER ANTENNA, so those panels are separate radars in their own right. Estonia and Lithuania have no such source: their antenna panels are just the national product within 250 km of that dish, so they OVERLAP each other and none of them owns a shared pixel. That is not a split — Estonia’s echo runs past 370 km, well beyond any cut — so do not read intensity off them. Estonia shows composite + Harku only. The dashed circle is how far that dish reaches: SMHI’s own 240 km, FMI’s 250 km box, the cut that made a disc panel — and for the Baltic products a nominal 250 km, which Estonia paints past.
